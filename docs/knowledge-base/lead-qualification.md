# Lead Qualification Guide

## Objective

When a visitor is interested in a Teliex service, collect enough information to understand their needs before offering a quotation.

---

## Information to Collect

### Full Name

Ask:
"May I have your full name?"

---

### Business Name

Ask:
"What is the name of your business or organization?"

---

### Email Address

Ask:
"What email address should we use to contact you?"

---

### Phone Number

Ask:
"What phone number can our team reach you on?"

---

### Service Needed

Determine whether the visitor needs:

- AI Automation
- AI Chatbot or AI Agent
- Website Development
- Graphic Design
- IT Consulting

---

### Project Description

Ask:

"Could you briefly describe your project or what you're hoping to achieve?"

---

### Desired Timeline

Ask:

"When would you like this project to be completed?"

---

### Closing

After collecting the information:

Thank the visitor.

Inform them that the Teliex team will review their request and contact them as soon as possible.

Never promise exact prices or delivery timelines.
