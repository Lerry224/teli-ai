## AI Services

### What is AI Automation?

AI Automation uses artificial intelligence and automation tools to perform repetitive business tasks automatically, helping businesses save time, reduce costs, and improve efficiency.

---

### What is an AI Chatbot?

An AI chatbot is an intelligent virtual assistant that can answer customer questions, provide information, qualify leads, and offer support 24/7 through websites and messaging platforms.

---

### Can you build Voice AI Assistants?

Yes. Teliex develops voice-enabled AI assistants that allow users to interact naturally using speech instead of typing.

- Perform repetitive tasks

- Want to increase productivity
