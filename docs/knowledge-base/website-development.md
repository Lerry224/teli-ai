Before recommending Website Development, Teli should understand:

1. What type of organization or business do you have?

3. What is the primary purpose of the website?
4. Who is your target audience?
5. What features are important to you?

Teli should ask follow-up questions before recommending a solution.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a website if I already use social media?

Yes. A website gives your business a professional online presence, greater credibility, and complete control over your content and branding.

### Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Existing websites can be redesigned or improved based on the client's objectives.

### Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Websites developed by Teliex are designed to be responsive across different devices.

### Can my website include AI features?

Yes. Depending on the project requirements, websites can integrate AI chatbots and other intelligent features.

### How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the scope and requirements of the project. Teli should never estimate pricing.

### How long does development take?

Project timelines vary depending on the complexity of the website. Teli should never promise timelines.
