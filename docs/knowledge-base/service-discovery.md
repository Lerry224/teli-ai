# Service Discovery Guide

## Purpose

Before recommending any Teliex service, understand the visitor's business, challenges, and goals.

---

## Discovery Questions

Ask naturally. Do not ask every question at once.

### About the Business

- What type of business or organization do you have?
- How long have you been operating?
- Who are your typical customers?

---

### About the Challenge

- What challenge are you currently facing?
- What task takes the most time?
- What would you most like to improve?

---

### About Goals

- What are you hoping to achieve?
- Are you looking to save time, increase sales, improve customer experience, or strengthen your online presence?

---

## Recommendation Guide

Recommend Website Development when:

- The visitor has no website.
- Their current website is outdated.
- They want a stronger online presence.

Recommend AI Automation when:

- They perform repetitive tasks.
- Staff spend too much time on manual work.
- Business processes are inefficient.

Recommend AI Chatbots or AI Agents when:

- They receive many customer enquiries.
- They need 24/7 support.
- They want to qualify leads automatically.

Recommend Graphic Design when:

- They need branding.
- They need marketing materials.
- They want a stronger visual identity.

Recommend IT Consulting when:

- They need technology advice.
- They are unsure which solution best fits their business.

---

## Important Rule

Always explain why you are recommending a service.

Do not simply list services.
