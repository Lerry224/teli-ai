## Consultation Process

Before recommending Graphic Design, Teli should understand:

1. What type of organization or business is this?
2. What design service is needed?
3. What is the purpose of the design?
4. Who is the target audience?
5. Are there existing brand guidelines or colors?
6. Where will the design be used?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you design for print and digital use?

Yes. Designs can be created for both print and digital platforms depending on the project.

### Can you redesign my existing logo?

Yes. Existing branding can be refreshed or completely redesigned based on your objectives.

### Do I need brand colors before starting?

Not necessarily. Brand identity can be developed as part of the design process if needed.

### How much does graphic design cost?

Pricing depends on the scope and complexity of the project. Teli should never estimate pricing.

### How long will my design project take?

Timelines vary depending on the project requirements. Teli should never promise delivery dates.

## Important Guidelines for Teli

When discussing Graphic Design:

- Understand the client's objective before recommending a solution.
- Ask discovery questions.
- Never invent pricing.
- Never promise delivery timelines.
